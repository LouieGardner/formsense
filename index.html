Getting a good result

  • Film from the side, level with your hips, 3 to 4 m away, with your whole body and the bar in view.
  • Prop the phone up rather than holding it, and keep other people out of shot or well away from you.
  • If no rep is found, tap the plate nearest the camera and FormSense measures the lift from the plate.
  • Set your height and plate size in Settings: they turn the video into real distances and bar speeds.

Common questions

Are my videos uploaded? No. Videos are analysed on your phone and never leave it. See the privacy policy.

Why does it say "may be inaccurate"? Some checks can't be measured well from the angle you filmed, or from the plate alone. They're still shown, with a warning, so you get the best answer available.

Is this medical advice? No. FormSense gives general coaching feedback. Stop if anything hurts and speak to a qualified coach or physio.
