Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

FormSense is made by Louie Gardner. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the FormSense app. The short version: your videos and results stay on your phone, and we don't collect any personal data.

Your videos

When you choose or record a video, FormSense analyses it on your phone. Videos are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

What is stored, and where

Each analysed lift saves its tracked movement, small still pictures from the video, your report, any answers you give to "Was this right?" and your settings (such as your height and plate size). All of this is stored only on your phone. Deleting a lift in History removes it; deleting the app removes everything.

What we collect

Nothing. FormSense has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not share data with anyone.

Downloads from other services

To analyse a video, the app downloads its body-tracking software from Google's and jsDelivr's servers, and the report loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any download, those services see your phone's internet address. No video, result or personal information is sent to them.

Sharing a lift

If you use "Share data" on a report, the app creates a file with that lift's tracked movement and results and hands it to your phone's share menu. Where it goes is up to you.

Camera and photos

FormSense asks for the camera to record a lift and for your photo library to open a video you choose. It only uses the videos you pick.

Children

FormSense is not aimed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete: everything is on your phone and under your control. You can still contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
